How charges are calculated

The first 1 kg is covered by the base charge. Every started kg after that adds the per-kg charge. The COD fee is a percentage of the cash-on-delivery amount. If the COD amount reaches a courier's free-delivery threshold, the delivery charge is waived (the COD fee still applies). Rates here are the ones you entered — check the courier's current rate card before you ship.
